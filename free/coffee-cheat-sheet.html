Golden rule: start from these ratios, then adjust to taste. A 1:15–1:17 coffee-to-water ratio works for most filter methods.

2 · Grind Size Guide

Espresso

  • Fine — like powdered sugar
  • Too coarse = sour, fast shots

Pour-over / Drip

  • Medium-fine to medium
  • Table salt texture

AeroPress

  • Medium
  • Forgiving — wide range OK

French press

  • Coarse — sea salt
  • Avoid fines (muddy cup)

Cold brew

  • Extra coarse
  • Almost crushed peppercorns

Moka pot

  • Medium-fine
  • Between espresso & filter

4 · Extraction Troubleshooting

Sour / sharp

  • Under-extracted
  • → grind finer, brew longer, hotter water

Bitter / harsh

  • Over-extracted
  • → grind coarser, shorten time, cooler water

Weak / watery

  • Too little coffee
  • → increase dose or grind finer

Hollow / bland

  • Channeling
  • → distribute grounds, level, even tamp

5 · 3-Step Quick Start (any method)

  1. Weigh & grind. Use the ratio chart; match grind to your method.
  2. Heat water to the right temperature; rinse paper filters to remove paper taste.
  3. Brew & taste. Time it, then adjust grind / ratio next cup using the fix chart.

6 · Care & Maintenance

  • Daily: rinse grinder burrs area; wipe steam wand.
  • Weekly: backflush espresso machine; wash French press / AeroPress.
  • Monthly: descale machine with citric acid or descaler tablets.
  • Store beans: airtight, cool, dark — never the freezer door.
