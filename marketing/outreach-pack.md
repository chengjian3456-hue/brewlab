## 一、HARO Pitch 模板（记者找专家源，免费高权威外链）

HARO = Help A Reporter Out。记者每天发选题征集，你回一句专业引语，被采用就得到带链接的媒体露出处（Forbes、EatThis、RealSimple 等）。
注册：helpareporter.com（免费）。每天盯 "Lifestyle / Food & Drink" 关键词：coffee, espresso, cold brew, home brewing。

**用于 HARO 的 3 段可复制引语（署名填你的名字 + "founder of BrewLab"）：**

> Q: 新手第一台咖啡机怎么选？
> "Most beginners overthink the machine and underthink the grinder. Spend 70% of your first $400 budget on a consistent grinder and a $150–300 espresso machine, not the other way around — grind consistency is what actually determines cup quality." — [你的名字], BrewLab

> Q: 在家做咖啡为什么越来越火？
> "Home brewing shifted from hobby to daily ritual because the gear got genuinely good and cheap. A $30 gooseneck kettle and a $25 V60 now produce café-tier pour-over, so the 'good coffee needs a café' assumption simply isn't true anymore." — [你的名字], BrewLab

> Q: 冷萃和热萃区别？
> "Cold brew isn't 'iced coffee' — it's a different extraction entirely. Steeping coarse grounds in cold water for 12–18 hours pulls almost no acidity, which is why people who hate bitter coffee love it." — [你的名字], BrewLab

**提交格式**：记者要的是「1–2 句可直接引用的话 + 你的姓名/头衔/网站」。别发长文，别带营销腔。

## 五、关键提醒
- HARO 与客座博客是**慢杠杆**（数周到数月），但外链一旦拿到，长期拉权重，和 Pinterest 的快流量互补。

- 你只需做「发邮件/注册 HARO/在社群贴链接」这几步；选题、模板、清单我全写好，直接复制。
